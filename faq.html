Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our wigs, delivery, payments, returns, and orders.

🚚 Do you deliver nationwide in South Africa?

Yes, we deliver across all provinces in South Africa using trusted courier services.

⏱️ How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes 2–5 business days depending on your location and courier schedules.

📱 Can I order through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can place orders directly through WhatsApp and our team will assist you with payment and delivery.

💳 Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept EFT, Instant EFT, debit cards, credit cards, and secure online payments.

🛍️ Can't find the product you want?

Contact us via WhatsApp or email with the details of the product you're looking for and we'll do our best to source it for you.

🔄 Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes. Returns or exchanges may be requested within 24 hours of receiving your order, provided the product remains in its original condition and packaging.

💇 Are your wigs human hair?

We offer both human hair and premium synthetic wigs depending on the product selected.

✨ Can I customize my wig?

Yes. Selected wigs can be customized according to length, style, density, and colour preferences.

🏢 Do you have a physical store?

Yes, we are based at 46 Santa Maria Flats, King Edward Street, Willows, Bloemfontein, 9302.

📧 How do I contact support?

You can contact us via WhatsApp, email, social media, or our contact form for assistance.

📦 When will I receive my order?

Orders are processed after payment confirmation. Courier Guy delivery takes approximately 2–3 working days, while PAXI options range from 3–9 working days depending on the selected service.

💰 Do you require full payment before shipping?

Yes. Orders are only processed and dispatched once full payment has been received and confirmed.
